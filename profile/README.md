# Aokumo AI

**Let AI act. Stay in control.**

Aokumo AI frees engineers from repetitive operations. AI agents investigate and resolve IT and security issues across cloud, Kubernetes and on-premises, so your team can focus on innovation.

## Governed Execution

Governed Execution makes AI safe to run in production:

- Checks the risk of every change before AI acts
- Follows your approval policies
- Verifies the result
- Gives every action an undo button, with rollback planned before it runs

**Infrastructure Intelligence** continuously maps your cloud, Kubernetes and security risks, so every decision starts from real context.

Model-agnostic, and connects with external AI agents.
